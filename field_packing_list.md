# Silverleaf Nightshade Field Packing & Shopping List

## 🛒 Amazon Shopping List (Consumables)
*Quantities are estimated for an 11-site trip (e.g. Kerrville -> Wichita Falls).*

1. **Jumbo Clear Plastic Bags (For Dr. Sotti's Bag-and-Snip)**
   - **Quantity to Buy:** 100 bags (You need ~44 exact)
   - **Amazon Search:** `"20x30 clear plastic poly bag 3 mil lay flat"`
   - **Also need:** 1 pack of 100 zip-ties to seal them at the bottom.
2. **15mL Falcon Tubes (For Dr. Kariyat's Pooled Genetics)**
   - **Quantity to Buy:** 50-pack (You need 33 exact)
   - **Amazon Search:** `"15mL sterile conical centrifuge tubes"`
3. **Dry Ice Shipping Coolers (For FedEx Overnight)**
   - **Quantity to Buy:** 4 coolers (One for each day of shipping, plus a backup)
   - **Amazon Search:** `"Insulated shipping box with cardboard outer 12x10x9"`
4. **Dry Ice Shipping Labels**
   - **Quantity to Buy:** 1 pack/roll
   - **Amazon Search:** `"UN1845 Dry Ice Labels Class 9"`
5. **Heavy Duty Ziplocks (For Dr. Sotti's Roots & Soil)**
   - **Quantity to Buy:** 100 bags (Usually sold in boxes of 30-40, so buy 3 boxes)
   - **Amazon Search:** `"Gallon freezer bags heavy duty"`
6. **Specimen Vials**
   - **Quantity to Buy:** 50 vials (For extra/loose insects outside the bag)
   - **Amazon Search:** `"Plastic specimen vials with leak-proof screw caps"` 
   - **Also need:** 1 bottle of 70% ethanol/rubbing alcohol to fill them.

## 🎒 Full Field Packing List (Tools & Hardware)

### Electronics & Tech
- [ ] **Smartphone:** Your primary camera and GPS logger (ensure location services/EXIF data are ON).
- [ ] **High-Capacity Power Bank & Cables:** Using the camera and GPS in the sun will drain your phone battery very fast.

### Field Hardware
- [ ] **Shovel / Spade:** For excavating the 20-30cm root balls.
- [ ] **0.5m x 0.5m Quadrat/Grid:** For density photos and random plant selection.
- [ ] **Measuring Tape or Ruler:** For plant height and scale reference in photos.
- [ ] **Scissors or Clippers:** For taking genetic leaf samples and snipping the main stem.
- [ ] **Rubbing Alcohol / Wipes:** To sterilize the clippers between plants and avoid cross-contamination.
- [ ] **Sharpie / Permanent Marker:** Alcohol/water-resistant markers for labeling all bags and vials.
- [ ] **Transparent Adhesive Tape (CLEAR TAPE):** Crucial! Wrap this over the Sharpie labels on the Falcon tubes so the ink doesn't freeze off in Liquid Nitrogen/Dry Ice.

### Coolers & Preservation
- [ ] **4 Dry Ice Shipping Coolers:** The small cardboard-lined styrofoam boxes you will hand to FedEx each night.
- [ ] **1 Master Dry Ice Holding Cooler:** A separate, thick styrofoam cooler (or an unlatched regular cooler) kept in the truck to hold your bulk dry ice stash. *Do not latch it airtight!*
- [ ] **1 or 2 Large Standard Coolers:** (e.g., Igloo or Yeti). Packed with regular ice/gel packs. This is for all the Jumbo Plastic Bags of plants/bugs, and the root/soil Ziplocks. *Do not put these on dry ice!*

### Field Comfort & Safety Accessories
- [ ] **Thick Leather Work Gloves:** Essential for handling the thorny Nightshade stems and protecting your hands during root excavation.
- [ ] **Hori Hori Knife (Serrated Trowel):** Highly recommended for sawing cleanly through tough Nightshade rhizomes.
- [ ] **Entomology Forceps (Tweezers):** Soft-tip tweezers for gently plucking insects without crushing them.
- [ ] **Foam Kneeling Pad:** To protect your knees from rocks and thorns while zip-tying plants.
- [ ] **Field Safety Gear:** Heavy-duty bug spray (DEET/Picaridin), sunscreen, wide-brim hat, ample drinking water, and snake gaiters (if working in wild pastures).
