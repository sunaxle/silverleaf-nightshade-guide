# Field and Lab Workflow Methodology
*The golden rule of field ecology: Do the absolute minimum while exposed to the elements. Collect, stabilize, label, and bring it back to the lab.*

---

## Part 1: The Bag Nomenclature (Labeling System)
A missing or smeared label ruins a sample. All bags and vials must be labeled using Sharpie (which is alcohol and water-resistant) with the following nomenclature:

**Format:** `[DATE]-[SITE]-[TREATMENT]-[PLANT]-[SAMPLE]`

**Code Breakdown:**
*   **DATE:** MMDD format (e.g., `0520` for May 20th).
*   **SITE:** The site number from your pre-planned sheet (e.g., `S01`).
*   **TREATMENT:** `D` for Disturbed (urban/roadside) or `U` for Undisturbed (wild pasture).
*   **PLANT:** The plant number at that site (e.g., `P01` through `P04`).
*   **SAMPLE TYPE:** `TOP`, `ROOTSOIL`, `BUG`.

**Examples:**
*   `0520-S01-D-P03-BUG`: May 20, Site 1, Disturbed, Plant 3, Insect Vial.
*   `0520-S01-D-P01-ROOTSOIL`: May 20, Site 1, Disturbed, Plant 1, Roots with Soil.

---

## Part 2: In-Field Workflow (Fast & Dirty)
*Time per site: ~30-45 minutes total for 4 plants per grid.*

1. **Digital Check-In & Site Measurements:** 
   - Get GPS location.
   - Estimate Soil Moisture.
   - Take pictures showing: neighboring vegetation, density of SLN, and herbivory damage (all types).
2. **Plant Selection:** 
   - Lay down a 0.5m x 0.5m (0.25 sq M) grid.
   - Randomly select 4 fully-grown (flowering/fruiting) plants. 
   - Try to select 2 with herbivory and 2 without herbivory. Include all types of herbivory.

### 2. Genetic Leaf Sampling & Dry Ice Logistics
**Objective**: Preserve SLNS leaf tissue for transcriptomic analysis (gene expression) without RNA degradation.
1. **Clipping**: Remove 3-4 young, fully expanded leaves per plant using sterilized scissors (to prevent cross-contamination).
2. **Flash Freezing**: Immediately place leaves into a pre-labeled sterile tube or Whirl-Pak bag and drop directly into the **Dry Ice Cooler** within 60 seconds of clipping.
3. **Chain of Custody**: Log the `Genetic_Sample_ID` in the Data Collection App, ensuring the "Sample Stored in Dry Ice" checkbox is ticked.

### 3. Whole Plant Harvesting (Shovel Method)
1. **Excavation:** Use a shovel to dig approximately 20-30cm around the root area. Try to get the entire plant intact.
2. **Above-Ground (Top):** Clip the top at ground level. Place the entire above-ground portion into a **Paper Bag**. Label it: `[Nomenclature]-TOP`.
3. **Below-Ground (Roots & Soil):** Place the roots along with the intact surrounding soil into a **Ziplock Bag**. Label it: `[Nomenclature]-ROOTSOIL`.
4. **Bugs:** Shake or pluck any live bugs into a vial. Label: `[Nomenclature]-BUG`.
5. **Stabilize:** Place all bug vials, root/soil Ziplocs, and genetic samples into coolers immediately.

## Part 3: In-Lab Workflow
*Processing the collected physical samples.*

1. **Leaf Trait Analysis:**
    - Calculate **Specific Leaf Area (SLA)**.
    - Measure **Leaf Thickness** (thick vs thin).
    - Analyze **Leaf Shape**.
    - Quantify **Trichome** density/type.
    - Determine **Leaf CN (Carbon/Nitrogen) content**.
2. **Whole Plant Metrics:**
    - Measure final **Height**.
    - Assess **Herbivory type and damage rating** on the foliage.
3. **Root and Soil Analysis:**
    - Extract soil from the root balls and test for **Soil CN content** and **pH**.
    - Stain and clear roots to quantify **AMF (Arbuscular Mycorrhizal Fungi)** and **DSE (Dark Septate Endophytes)** colonization.
4. **Insect Processing:**
    - Place bug vials in the freezer for 24 hours to euthanize safely.
    - Identify and log counts into the master database.
