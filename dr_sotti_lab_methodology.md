# Dr. Sotti's Lab & Field Methodology

*This document outlines the specific sampling grid and laboratory protocols established by Dr. Sotti for the Silverleaf Nightshade (SLN) project.*

## 1. On-Site Field Measurements
Before extracting plants, collect the overarching grid metadata:
1. **Soil Moisture** Estimate
2. **Geolocation** (GPS via Data Collection App)
3. **Photography**: Take clear pictures from 1.5m high, pointing straight down at the quadrat to show:
   - Neighboring vegetation
   - Density of SLN
   - Herbivory damage (all types)

## 2. SLN Plant Sampling (The Grid Method)
Instead of a transect line, utilize a specific grid geometry.

### 2.1 The Grid
- Establish a **0.5m x 0.5m (0.25 sq M)** sampling grid at the site.

### 2.2 Plant Extraction
- Within the 0.25 sq M grid, randomly select **4 plants**.
- **Condition:** Try to make sure these plants are fully grown (flowering/fruiting).
- **Herbivory Ratio:** When selecting the plants, if you see herbivory in some, sample **2 with herbivory and 2 without**. Include all types of herbivory.
- **Extraction:** Use a shovel to dig approximately 20-30cm around the root area and try to get the entire plant.

### 2.3 Bagging and Separation
- Clip the top at ground level and place in a bag.
- Place the roots with the surrounding soil in a different Ziplock bag and label appropriately using the established nomenclature.

---

## 3. Laboratory Analysis Protocol
Once back in the lab, the following metrics must be analyzed and recorded into the dataset for each plant:

### 3.1 Leaf Traits
- **Specific Leaf Area (SLA)**
- **Leaf Thickness** (is the leaf thick or thin)
- **Shape**
- **Trichomes** (Density/Type)
- **Leaf CN (Carbon/Nitrogen) Content**

### 3.2 Whole Plant Traits
- **Height**
- **Herbivory Type and Damage Rating**

### 3.3 Root & Soil Ecology
- **Soil CN Content**
- **pH**
- **AMF (Arbuscular Mycorrhizal Fungi) in roots**
- **DSE (Dark Septate Endophytes) in roots**
