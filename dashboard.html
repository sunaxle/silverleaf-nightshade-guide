Testable Hypotheses Derived from Dataset

These hypotheses link the independent ecological variables to the multi-trophic dependent variables collected in the database.

Hypothesis Title The Ecological Concept How to Test with this Dataset
1. The Fungal Disruption Hypothesis
Below-Ground Above-Ground 		Silverleaf nightshade relies on its root system. If disturbed roadside soils disrupt native Arbuscular Mycorrhizal Fungi (AMF) networks, the plant becomes biochemically weaker against insects. Correlate Treatment (Disturbed vs Undisturbed) with Soil_AMF_Spore_Count_Site_Avg and Lab_Root_AMF_Colonization_Pct. Then, cross-reference low AMF values with higher Lab_Larvae_Count to prove disrupted fungi leads to higher insect loads.
2. Life-Stage vs. Fecundity Breakdown
Above-Ground 		Counting just "adult bugs" doesn't reflect population dynamics. Highly stressed plants might attract adults, but fail to support larval development. Compare Field_Drought_Stress or Field_Damage_Score against the ratio of Lab_Adult_Bugs to Lab_Egg_Count. Does high stress halt reproduction?
3. The Spines vs. Fungi Trade-off
Below-Ground Above-Ground 		Plants have a limited energy budget. They can invest in below-ground fungal networks OR above-ground mechanical defenses, but rarely both. Perform a regression analysis comparing Lab_Spine_Density_0_to_2 against Lab_Root_AMF_Colonization_Pct. Look for an inverse relationship (High AMF = Low Spines).
4. The "Enemy Release" & Genetic Origin
Genetics Above-Ground 		If the Rio Grande Valley is the true evolutionary origin, populations there should have the highest baseline defenses because they evolved alongside specific predators. Invading populations (Panhandle) "escaped" and lost defenses. Compare Ecoregion (Valley vs Panhandle) against Lab_Spine_Density and Lab_Leaf_CN_Ratio. If leaf DNA sequencing is added to the dataset, correlate maximum genetic diversity with the Southern ecoregion.
5. Multi-Trophic Biocontrol Synergy
Below-Ground Above-Ground 		Below-ground herbivores (Nematodes) weaken the plant systemically, making it highly susceptible to extreme defoliation by above-ground Beetles. Look for a synergistic spike: Are the highest Field_Damage_Score values found exclusively on plants that *also* have severe Soil_Nematode_Count_Site_Avg pressure?
6. Environmental Proximity & Transcriptomic Stress
Genetics Above-Ground 		Proximity to heavily disturbed areas (like active agricultural fields) induces chronic stress pathways in SLNS, fundamentally altering its transcriptomic profile before herbivores even arrive. Correlate Proximity_Metrics with Genetic_Expression_Notes. If plants marked as "Agricultural Field" consistently show "Upregulated defense pathways", we can prove that proximity alone induces a preemptive defense state.
