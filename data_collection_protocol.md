# Field Data Collection Protocol (Fused Checklist)

## Phase 1: Preparation & Arrival
- [ ] 1. **Pack Equipment:** Ensure you have your smartphone, GPS, measuring tape, 0.5m x 0.5m grid, shovel, Jumbo plastic bags, ziplock bags, 15mL Falcon tubes, specimen vials, Sharpie, clear tape, and Coolers.
- [ ] 2. **Arrive at Site:** Identify a suitable Silverleaf Nightshade patch.
- [ ] 3. **Log Site Data:** Open the Data Collection App and record the overarching site details (Site Name, Weather, Date/Time).

## Phase 2: Grid Setup & Environmental Data
- [ ] 4. **Place Grid:** Lay down the **0.5m x 0.5m (0.25 sq M)** sampling grid over a representative cluster of SLN plants.
- [ ] 5. **Log Geolocation:** Record GPS coordinates using the app.
- [ ] 6. **Estimate Soil Moisture:** Record current soil moisture (Dry, Moist, Saturated) in the app.
- [ ] 7. **Assess Ecology:** Note proximity metrics (Roadside, Ag Field, etc.) and any co-occurring vegetation in the app.
- [ ] 8. **Take Onsite Photos (Per Kariyat's Instructions):** 
    - **Neighbor Vegetation & Context:** Take a wide landscape photo showing the grid and the surrounding area.
    - **SNL Density:** Stand facing North, hold the camera **1.5 meters high**, and point straight down at the grid.
    - **Herbivore Damage:** Take close-up photos of any visible damage on the plants within the grid.

## Phase 3: Dr. Kariyat’s RNA-seq Protocol (9 Plants, Pooled)
*Target: 9 plants at the exact same developmental stage per site. Do not mix stages or populations.*
- [ ] 9. **Pre-Label Tubes:** Label **three 15mL Falcon tubes** (Replicate 1, 2, and 3) with a Sharpie: Population ID, stage, rep number, date, and initials. Immediately wrap the tube in clear adhesive tape over the writing.
- [ ] 10. **Replicate 1:** Find 3 plants. Cut exactly 2 top fully expanded leaves from each (6 leaves total). Stuff all 6 leaves into the Replicate 1 tube.
- [ ] 11. **Snap Freeze:** Drop the tube immediately into the Liquid Nitrogen dewar (or Dry Ice cooler, if approved).
- [ ] 12. **Replicate 2 & 3:** Repeat steps 10-11 for Replicate 2 (3 new plants) and Replicate 3 (3 new plants).
*(Total: 3 tubes, 9 plants sampled).*

## Phase 4: Dr. Sotti’s Harvest Protocol (4 Plants, Full Excavation)
*Target: 4 representative plants. (We recommend NOT using the same 9 plants you just clipped).*
- [ ] 13. **Select 4 Plants:** Randomly select 4 plants within or near the grid.
- [ ] 14. **Metrics:** Measure the plant height (cm) and log the visual bug damage score (0-4) in your app.
- [ ] 15. **Observe Insects:** Quickly log any visible insects before bagging.
- [ ] 16. **Bag and Snip (Top/Insects):** 
    - Carefully slip a **Jumbo Clear Poly Bag (3 mil)** over the entire above-ground portion of the plant.
    - Zip tie the bag tightly around the base of the stem at ground level to trap all insects inside.
    - Snip the stem just below the zip tie.
    - Label the bag `[DATE]-[SITE]-[TREATMENT]-[PLANT]-TOP` and store in the regular cooler.
- [ ] 17. **Excavate Roots/Soil (Bottom):** Use a shovel to dig 20-30cm around the remaining root area. Extract the root ball intact. Place the roots and surrounding soil into a **Ziplock Bag**. Label `[DATE]-[SITE]-[TREATMENT]-[PLANT]-ROOTSOIL`. Store in regular cooler.
- [ ] 18. **Repeat:** Repeat steps 14-17 for the remaining 3 plants.

## Phase 5: Post-Field Processing
- [ ] 19. **Sync Data:** Ensure all Data Collection App records are successfully synced.
- [ ] 20. **Cross-Reference:** Ensure every physical bag/vial matches a digital record.
- [ ] 21. **Ship Genetics:** Prepare the Dry Ice samples (Falcon tubes) for FedEx Express shipment. Do not tape the cardboard box airtight.
