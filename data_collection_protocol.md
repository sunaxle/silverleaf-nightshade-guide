# Field Data Collection Protocol

## 1. Preparation
Before heading to the field, ensure you have the following equipment:
- Smartphone/Tablet with access to the Data Collection App (or printed sheets as backup).
- GPS device (if not using smartphone GPS).
- Measuring tape (for plant height/density).
- Specimen collection vials with 70% ethanol (for unidentifiable insects).
- Notebook and permanent marker.
- Camera/Smartphone for taking voucher photographs.

## 2. Site Selection and Setup
1. Identify a Silverleaf Nightshade patch.
2. Record the overarching site details (Site Name, Weather, Date/Time).
3. Establish a standard observation plot (e.g., 1x1 meter quadrat) or use a transect line depending on patch size.

## 3. Data Collection Steps
For each sampled plant or quadrat, record the following metrics in the Data Collection App:

### 3.1 Plant Metrics
- **Plant Height:** Measure from soil to the highest growing point (cm).
- **Phenological Stage:** Vegetative, Budding, Flowering, Fruiting, or Senescing.
- **Plant Health/Damage Score:** 
  - 0: No damage
  - 1: 1-25% foliar damage
  - 2: 26-50% damage
  - 3: 51-75% damage
  - 4: >75% damage or dead

### 3.2 Below-Ground & Ecological Metrics
- **Co-occurring Vegetation / Crops:** Note the surrounding plant species. *Important for identifying nematode/pest reservoirs.*
- **Field Soil Moisture Estimate:** Dry, Moist, or Saturated.
- **Rhizosphere Soil Sample:** Use a trowel to collect soil directly surrounding the roots into a ziploc bag. Store in a cooler. Label bag and record the **Soil Sample ID**.
- **Root Sample Collected?:** Check box if a subset of the root system was collected for lab AMF/nematode staining.

### 3.3 Insect Observations
- **Insect Type:** Common Name or Scientific Name (use Field Guidebook for reference).
- **Count:** Number of individuals observed on the plant.
- **Life Stage:** Egg, Larva/Nymph, Pupa, Adult.
- **Behavior:** Feeding, Mating, Resting, Predation.

### 3.3 Media and Location
- **GPS Coordinates:** Log current latitude and longitude.
- **Photographs:** Take at least one photo of the whole plant and one macro photo of any insects found. Note the photo ID or upload directly.

## 4. Specimen Collection (If applicable)
- If an insect cannot be identified in the field:
  - Capture using a sweep net or aspirator.
  - Transfer to a vial.
  - Label vial with: Date, Site Name, GPS coordinates, and Plant ID.

## 5. Post-Field Processing
- Ensure all data from the app is successfully synced to Google Sheets.
- Cross-reference physical specimens with digital records.
- Identify unknown specimens using laboratory resources.
